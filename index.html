About

I am a PhD candidate at the Institute of Medical Informatics at RWTH Aachen University. Previously, I carried out research at Research Center Jülich and at the Charité in Berlin. I received my MSc (2022) and BSc (2019) in Biological Information Processing from RWTH Aachen.

I develop and evaluate machine learning methods for clinical data. My current focus is the deployment and monitoring of prediction models in clinical settings; other work includes seizure detection with wearable ECG and LLM-based feedback in medical education.

Research Experience

PhD Researcher

Institute of Medical Informatics, RWTH Aachen University, Aachen, Germany

2022 – Present
  • MoniPy. Developed a seizure-detection method for wearable ECG combining heart-rate variability features, automatic data quality assessment, and a deep learning classifier.
  • HaMoJo. Built a local-LLM pipeline that turns audio and video recordings of paediatric simulation training into structured feedback reports, using speech recognition, video-based behavior analysis, and LLM scoring.
  • FORECAST (AKTIN). Developing machine learning models to forecast hospital resource use, such as inpatient length of stay, from routine emergency department data, and deployed them in the clinical setting with ongoing performance monitoring.
